<u>PyGame</u><br />
<a href="https://github.com/pygame/pygame">PyGame</a> is a free and open-source cross-platform library for the development of video games using Python. Using Simple <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_DirectMedia_Layer">DirectMedia Layer</a> to abstract most common functions makes it easier to write these programs.
