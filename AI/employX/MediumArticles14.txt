REMEMBER
Training
learning phase where a model studies massive amounts of data and adjusts its internal weights
think: studying for an exam

Inference [Prediction]
active phase where the already-trained model takes new, unseen data and produces an output, answer or score
without changing its learned weights
think: sit the exam and answering questions
