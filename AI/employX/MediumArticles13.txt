The Better AI Gets at Coding, the More Developers We Will Need

AI makes software development cheaper → more software becomes economically worthwhile to build → the total amount of software we build increases → therefore the demand for people who can build, direct, maintain and understand that software can increase.



JEV
Jevons paradox: when you make a resource more efficient/cheaper, you can accidentally create more demand for the resource.
