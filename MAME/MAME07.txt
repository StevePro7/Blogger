Follow on from MAME 06


When the MAME debugger launches
in the console window at the bottom we can enter commands


e.g.
dasm wboy.asm,0,10000,0
Disassembles the ROM without opcodes

OR
dasm wboy_with_opcodes.asm,0,10000,1
dasm wboy_with_opcodes.asm,0,10000

Disassembles the ROM with opcodes


AND
save wboy_maincpu.bin,maincpu,10000
Dumps out the entire ROM but the opcodes are encoded
i.e. raw program / data ROM

Whereas
saveo wboy_decrypted_opcodes.bin,0,10000

exports the byte stream that corresponds to the debugger's disassembly
Decrypted instruction/opcode view
i.e.
saveo	= save Opcodes
